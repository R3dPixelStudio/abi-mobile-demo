# Catalog, image rights, and editorial sources

## Current local revision - 2026-10-29

The current catalog contains 92 products (20 phones, 72 accessories including seven watches/bands). All prices, stock and offers are illustrative integer Toman values. Generic accessories and fitted case/glass records use original schematic artwork; branded records use limited official specifications. Supplier SKU, fit, regional configuration and media rights require confirmation before real sales. The older inventory totals below describe the original seed only.

`src/data/catalog-media.json` records additional optimized reference images, original URLs, official source pages, dimensions and transformations. `catalog-photo-credits.json` records three additional CC BY-SA photographs with authors and licenses. /credits exposes attribution and source links. Crops isolate actual model imagery, not invented alternate angles. The Pixel 9 Pro crop isolates the smaller Pro from the official Pro/Pro XL comparison; mixed-model originals are excluded from its gallery.

Manufacturer references have **no asserted open license or established reuse permission**. They are included for local design review. Obtain approved supplier/manufacturer media before public publication or production use. Existing CC media retain their attribution and license requirements, including ShareAlike where applicable. The owner's Sketchfab model retains its supplied Standard License (public/models/iphone-18/license.txt).

All 20 phones now have model-reference imagery and 21 specification fields linked to official documentation through referenceUrl. Eighteen phones have multiple gallery images; S23 FE and A55 currently have one each. The seven unavailable-image cards described below are no longer used. Wikimedia rate limits were respected and requests stopped. Watch, audio and power galleries have one to three references depending on availability. Original schematic SVGs remain illustrative.
